What is CampusBite?

CampusBite is a student-centered digital cafeteria menu and ordering portal. It gives college students a quick, transparent, and refined way to browse available food, check exact menu prices, and calculate meal expenses effortlessly.

💡

Why Was It Developed?

During busy recess and lunch breaks, college canteens face severe rush and long queues. CampusBite enables students to pre-calculate their orders and pick up meals swiftly at Counter #1.

⚡

How Does It Work?

Browse daily culinary items, add your favorite dishes to your food tray, and preview an instant automatic bill calculation. Receive a counter pickup token to collect your hot meal quickly at Counter #1.
