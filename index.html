About Campus Café

Your Friendly Campus Food Hub

🏫

What is Campus Café?

Campus Café is a student-centered digital cafeteria menu and ordering prototype. It is designed to give college students a quick, transparent, and enjoyable way to browse available food, check exact menu prices, and calculate their food expenses.

💡

Why Was It Developed?

During busy recess and lunch breaks, college canteens face severe rush and long queues. Campus Café enables students to easily help students pre-calculate their order and streamline cafeteria counters.

⚡

How Does It Work?

Browse daily menu items, add your favorite dishes to your food tray, and preview an instant automatic bill calculation. Receive a counter pickup token to collect your hot meal quickly at Counter #1.
